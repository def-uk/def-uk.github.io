# I experiment, build, and set things in motion.

Hi, I'm **Dongwook**. I experiment with machine learning models, from decision trees to deep learning vision models. I like comparing models under the same conditions and narrowing down why one underperforms with data and experiments. I also build apps and websites with Flutter and React, and make video and motion with After Effects and Premiere.

This is the studio where that work comes together. Projects live in **Work**, and notes from studying and experimenting live in **Log**.
